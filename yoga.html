Yoga for Relaxation

Follow these simple steps to start your yoga journey. 🧘‍♂️

Simple Yoga Steps

  1. Breathing Exercise: Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and take deep breaths. Focus on inhaling and exhaling slowly.
  2. Child's Pose: Kneel on the floor, stretch your arms forward, and rest your forehead on the mat. Hold for 30 seconds.
  3. Downward Dog: Place your hands and feet on the ground, lift your hips, and create an inverted "V" shape.
  4. Tree Pose: Stand on one foot, place the other foot on your inner thigh, and balance with your hands above your head.
  5. Final Relaxation: Lie down flat on your back, close your eyes, and relax every part of your body.
